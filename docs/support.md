# KoF22 Postr support

KoF22 Postr publishes short posts, photos and links to [kof22.com](https://www.kof22.com).

## Getting started

1. Open the app. A fresh install starts in **Mock** mode: tap **Sign in (mock)** to try
   everything without an account; nothing leaves your device.
2. To publish for real, choose **api.kof22.com** on the sign-in screen (or in Settings) and
   sign in with your KoF22 account. Publishing needs an account with publish rights.
3. Write a post, add photos (tap a photo to add alt text or keep its location), tags, a
   link or a place, then **Post**. Posts written offline wait in the **Outbox** and send
   automatically.

## Troubleshooting

- **"This account can't publish":** the account needs publish rights on kof22.com.
- **Sign-in or connection problems:** open **Settings → Diagnostics** to check the sign-in
  service and the API.
- **A post is stuck in the Outbox:** pull to refresh or tap **Send now**; failed items show
  the reason and a Retry button.
